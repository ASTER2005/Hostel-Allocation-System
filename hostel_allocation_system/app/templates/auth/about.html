Hostel Allocation Guidelines & Regulations

Official policies governing accommodation applications, room allotments, and resident responsibilities.

1. Allocation Business Logic & Eligibility

Hostel allotment is governed by computational fairness rules enforced directly by the database architecture:

  • Single Active Application: A student cannot have multiple concurrent accommodation applications. Submitting a new application requires prior resolution of pending requests.
  • Single Active Allocation: Under no circumstances can a resident hold multiple active room assignments.
  • Strict Capacity Thresholds: Rooms cannot exceed their designated structural capacities (Single = 1, Double = 2, Triple = 3, Dormitory = 4-6). Database transactions reject over-allocation atomically.
  • Gender Segregated Halls: Boys halls are strictly reserved for male scholars; Girls halls are restricted to female scholars; Co-ed facilities accommodate registered research scholars.
  • Administrative Verification: Room allocation requires formal approval by the Dean of Student Affairs before wardens can execute check-in.

2. Physical Check-in & Check-out Process

Once an application is approved and a room allocated:

Arrival Check-in

Scholars report to their hostel warden's office with college ID. The warden marks the student as 'Checked-In' on the live database sheet.

Term Check-out

When vacating or graduating, the warden records the checkout date. This automatically decrements room occupancy and releases the bed.

3. Maintenance & Grievance Redressal

Scholars can log maintenance tickets (electrical, plumbing, Wi-Fi, carpentry) directly from their student dashboard. Wardens update ticket progress in real-time, providing transparency and accountability.
